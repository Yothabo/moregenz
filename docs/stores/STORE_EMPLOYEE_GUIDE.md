## 1. Purpose

This guide explains how employees of participating stores are expected to use the platform responsibly and within their assigned permissions.

It establishes common expectations for account security, daily operations, customer information, product handling, inventory, orders and incident reporting.

The guide applies to employees who receive access to a participating store's administrative or operational functions.

## 2. Employee Access

Employees receive access through an authorised account and an active membership of their assigned store.

Access must be based on actual job responsibilities.

Employees must:

- Use their own accounts.
- Keep authentication credentials confidential.
- Follow assigned permission boundaries.
- Use only approved platform functions.
- Report access problems promptly.
- Avoid accessing information unrelated to their responsibilities.

An employee must never use another person's account to perform an action.

## 3. Permission Boundaries

Employee permissions are assigned through authorised store administration.

Available actions depend on the employee's assigned role and permissions.

Employees must not:

- Grant themselves additional permissions.
- Change another employee's access without authority.
- Access another store's information.
- Bypass application or backend security controls.
- Use restricted platform administration functions.
- Share private integration credentials.
- Attempt to conceal or alter recorded activity.

Requests for additional access must be submitted to the Store Admin.

## 4. Account Security

Employees must protect their accounts and devices.

They should:

- Use strong, unique passwords where applicable.
- Enable additional authentication controls where supported or required.
- Avoid sharing devices or leaving sessions unattended.
- Sign out from shared or public devices.
- Report suspicious authentication activity.
- Report lost devices that may contain active platform sessions.
- Avoid storing passwords in unsecured files or messages.

Suspected account compromise must be reported immediately.

## 5. Daily Platform Use

Employees should use the platform only for authorised store activities.

Before completing an important action, verify:

- The correct store is selected.
- The correct customer or order is open.
- Product and quantity information is accurate.
- The action is within assigned authority.
- Required approvals have been obtained.

Employees must not assume that an action succeeded merely because a button was selected.

Where the outcome is uncertain, check the recorded transaction status before repeating the action.

## 6. Product Information

Employees responsible for product information must use accurate and authorised information.

They must:

- Verify product identity.
- Use approved descriptions and images.
- Check current prices and availability.
- Report inaccurate product information.
- Avoid changing source product information without authority.
- Follow store procedures for product activation and deactivation.

Store-level listing permissions do not automatically grant ownership of shared catalogue information.

## 7. Inventory Handling

Employees assigned inventory responsibilities must record stock activity accurately.

Activities may include:

- Recording authorised stock receipts.
- Checking physical quantities.
- Reporting damaged or missing stock.
- Recording approved adjustments.
- Monitoring reserved stock.
- Preparing authorised stock movements.
- Reporting discrepancies.

Employees must not create artificial stock movements to hide shortages or alter inventory records without an authorised reason.

Stock belonging to another store must not be treated as available inventory.

## 8. Order Processing

Employees assigned order responsibilities must follow the store's fulfilment procedures.

They should:

- Verify order details.
- Confirm product availability.
- Follow authorised picking and packing procedures.
- Update supported fulfilment statuses accurately.
- Escalate missing or damaged items.
- Handle customer requests within their permissions.
- Report suspected fraudulent activity.

Payment must be treated as confirmed only when verified through the platform's trusted payment records.

## 9. Customer Information

Customer information must be accessed only for legitimate store activities.

Employees must:

- Use customer details only for authorised purposes.
- Avoid unnecessary collection of personal information.
- Verify the recipient before sharing customer information.
- Protect contact details and order history.
- Respect communication preferences.
- Report suspected privacy incidents.
- Avoid copying customer records into personal applications or accounts.

Customer information must not be used for personal marketing or unrelated activities.

## 10. Customer Communication

Employees communicating with customers must provide accurate and respectful information.

They must not:

- Promise unavailable products or unsupported services.
- Confirm unverified payments.
- Make unauthorised refund commitments.
- Disclose another customer's information.
- Misrepresent store policies.
- Send marketing communications without the required authorisation and consent.

Customer complaints and requests outside employee authority must be escalated to the Store Admin.

## 11. Financial Activities

Only employees with the relevant permissions may access financial functions.

Employees must:

- Follow approved payment and refund procedures.
- Verify transaction details before taking action.
- Preserve supporting evidence.
- Report suspicious transactions.
- Escalate unexplained discrepancies.
- Avoid making unauthorised adjustments.

Employees must never request or store full payment card details or security codes outside approved payment-provider processes.

## 12. Communication and Notifications

Employees must use approved communication channels for store operations.

They must protect sensitive information when communicating and avoid sharing internal operational details with unauthorised parties.

Customer notifications must follow store and platform communication policies.

## 13. Errors and Operational Problems

Employees must report:

- Incorrect product information.
- Inventory discrepancies.
- Duplicate or missing orders.
- Unclear payment outcomes.
- Failed notifications.
- Integration errors.
- Unexpected access restrictions.
- Suspected data exposure.
- System outages.

Employees must not repeatedly retry uncertain transactions without checking their status.

## 14. Incident Reporting

Employees must immediately report suspected:

- Account compromise.
- Unauthorised access.
- Customer data exposure.
- Financial fraud.
- Missing or manipulated records.
- Cross-store information exposure.
- Malicious activity.
- Significant service disruption.

Reports should include what happened, when it happened, the affected operation and any available reference identifiers.

Employees must not delete evidence or attempt unauthorised investigation.

## 15. Recordkeeping and Accountability

Material activities may be recorded in the platform's audit history.

Employees must understand that their actions are attributable to their individual accounts.

They must not attempt to:

- Share accounts to obscure responsibility.
- Alter audit history.
- Delete required operational evidence.
- Circumvent approval workflows.
- Misrepresent completed work.

## 16. Training and Guidance

Employees must receive appropriate guidance for the functions they are authorised to use.

Store Admins are responsible for ensuring that employees understand relevant procedures and permission boundaries.

Employees should request clarification before performing unfamiliar or high-risk actions.

## 17. Leaving or Changing Roles

When an employee changes responsibilities or leaves a store, their access must be reviewed and updated promptly.

Employees must return or surrender store-owned devices, documents and other resources according to store procedures.

They must not retain customer records, private credentials or confidential business information without authority.

## 18. Employee Responsibilities

Every employee is responsible for:

- Protecting their account.
- Following assigned permissions.
- Maintaining accurate records.
- Respecting customer privacy.
- Handling products and stock responsibly.
- Following order and payment procedures.
- Reporting incidents promptly.
- Cooperating with authorised reviews.
- Protecting confidential platform and store information.

## 19. Constraints

This guide does not grant additional permissions or replace formal employment agreements, store policies or platform governance requirements.

Actual platform capabilities depend on implemented functionality and assigned permissions.

Where an operation is not supported, employees must follow the authorised store procedure rather than bypassing platform controls.
