# Store Owner Community Programme Funding and Sponsorship Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how the parent company funds, sponsors and financially administers the Store Owner Community and Entrepreneur Development Programme.

The programme MUST have transparent funding arrangements and MUST NOT create undisclosed financial obligations for Store Owners.

## 2. Funding Sources

The programme MAY be funded through:
- An approved parent-company operating budget.
- Voluntary external sponsorships.
- Approved event partnerships.
- Grants or development funding.
- Voluntary event participation fees.
- Approved contributions from participating businesses.

Each funding source MUST have an identified owner, purpose and approval record.

The programme MUST NOT assume that subscription revenue automatically includes unlimited event attendance, travel, accommodation, catering or individual business-development services.

## 3. Annual Programme Budget

The programme administrator MUST prepare an annual budget for approval.

The budget SHOULD distinguish:
- Monthly networking activities.
- Quarterly workshops.
- Annual gatherings.
- Competition prizes.
- Mentorship activities.
- Learning materials and recordings.
- Venue and technology costs.
- Marketing and communications.
- Contingency expenditure.

The approved budget MUST identify funding sources and spending limits.

Actual expenditure MUST be reconciled against the approved budget.

## 4. Event-Level Financial Controls

Every event with financial commitments MUST have an approved cost estimate.

The event organiser MUST:
- Obtain approval before making commitments.
- Use authorised suppliers.
- Record invoices and supporting evidence.
- Track actual expenditure.
- Report material budget variances.
- Reconcile sponsorship funds and event income.

Unauthorised spending MUST NOT be represented as an approved programme expense.

## 5. Sponsorship Approval

External organisations MAY sponsor approved programme activities.

Before accepting sponsorship, the parent company MUST assess:
- Sponsor identity and reputation.
- Business relevance.
- Proposed financial or non-financial contribution.
- Sponsorship conditions.
- Conflicts of interest.
- Data and privacy implications.
- Brand and publicity requirements.
- Potential impact on Store Owner independence.

A sponsorship agreement MUST be approved by an authorised representative.

## 6. Sponsorship Benefits

Approved sponsorship benefits MAY include:
- Acknowledgement at an event.
- Approved logo placement.
- A presentation opportunity.
- An exhibition or information stand.
- Approved learning resources.
- Product or service demonstrations.

All benefits MUST be specified in writing.

Sponsors MUST NOT receive automatic access to Store Owner accounts, private discussions, customer information, sales data, supplier records or contact lists.

## 7. Independence and Commercial Protection

Sponsorship MUST NOT:
- Require Store Owners to purchase a sponsor's products.
- Force Store Owners into supplier agreements.
- Give sponsors control over competition outcomes.
- Give sponsors access to confidential business information.
- Prevent Store Owners from choosing competing suppliers.
- Give sponsors authority over platform governance.

Any sponsor recommendation or commercial offer MUST be clearly identified as such.

Store Owners MUST remain free to accept or reject sponsor offers.

## 8. Sponsor Participation

Sponsors MAY participate as speakers, exhibitors, facilitators or resource contributors when approved.

Their participation MUST have a defined purpose and duration.

Presentations MUST be relevant to the programme and MUST distinguish educational content from product promotion.

The parent company MAY remove sponsor participation where contractual, ethical, reputational or operational concerns arise, subject to the agreement.

## 9. Participation Fees

If an event requires a participation fee, the amount and conditions MUST be disclosed before registration.

The disclosure MUST state:
- What the fee covers.
- Whether payment is refundable.
- Applicable cancellation conditions.
- Whether attendance is optional.
- Whether alternative participation is available.

A Store Owner MUST NOT be automatically charged for registering or attending unless an applicable fee was disclosed and accepted.

## 10. Financial Records and Reporting

The parent company MUST maintain records of:
- Approved programme budgets.
- Sponsorship agreements.
- Sponsor contributions.
- Event income.
- Supplier expenditure.
- Prize commitments.
- Refunds.
- Budget variances.

Management reporting SHOULD show programme costs, funding sources and financial outcomes.

Store Owners MUST NOT receive access to confidential parent-company financial records beyond information authorised for community reporting.

## 11. Conflicts of Interest

Anyone approving sponsorships, suppliers, prizes or programme expenditure MUST disclose relevant personal or financial interests.

A conflicted decision-maker MUST NOT independently approve the affected transaction.

Conflicts and their resolutions MUST be recorded.

## 12. Acceptance Criteria

This policy is satisfied when:

1. Programme funding sources are documented.
2. Annual and event budgets require approval.
3. Financial commitments are authorised before being made.
4. Sponsorship terms are recorded in writing.
5. Sponsor access to data and participants is controlled.
6. Store Owner commercial independence is protected.
7. Participation fees are disclosed before acceptance.
8. Programme income and expenditure are auditable.
9. Conflicts of interest are declared and managed.
10. Sponsorship does not override platform governance or unified organisational authority.
