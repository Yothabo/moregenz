## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the rules and enforcement processes for maintaining a professional, respectful and constructive Store Owner community.

It applies to community profiles, discussions, messages, events, knowledge contributions, mentorship, collaboration listings and competitions.

It MUST operate consistently with the Store Owner Conduct, Compliance and Enforcement Policy.

## 2. Community Standards

Every participant MUST:
- Treat other members professionally and respectfully.
- Respect differences in business size, experience and resources.
- Share information honestly.
- Respect confidentiality and intellectual property.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation and discrimination.
- Respect another member's right to decline contact or collaboration.
- Follow published event and competition rules.

The community MUST encourage constructive disagreement, questions and exchange of different business experiences.

## 3. Prohibited Conduct

The platform MUST prohibit:
- Harassment, threats, intimidation and targeted abuse.
- Fraudulent business claims or fabricated achievements.
- Impersonation and identity misrepresentation.
- Spam and repeated unsolicited promotions.
- Publishing private information without authorisation.
- Sharing another store's confidential commercial information.
- Plagiarism or unauthorised use of intellectual property.
- Manipulating competition entries or judging.
- Coercive sales, investment or supplier pressure.
- Retaliation against members who report misconduct.
- Attempts to bypass community access restrictions.

## 4. Reporting

Members MUST be able to report:
- Profiles.
- Discussions and replies.
- Messages where supported.
- Knowledge resources.
- Collaboration listings.
- Mentorship conduct.
- Competition misconduct.
- Event-related behaviour.

A report MUST capture:
- Report identifier.
- Reporting member.
- Affected content or activity.
- Report category.
- Description.
- Submission timestamp.
- Review status.
- Assigned reviewer.
- Resolution.

The reporting interface MUST explain that submitting a report does not guarantee a particular outcome.

## 5. Moderation Workflow

The moderation process MUST support:

1. Report submitted.
2. Report acknowledged.
3. Initial assessment.
4. Reviewer assignment.
5. Evidence review.
6. Decision.
7. Action or closure.
8. Notification to affected parties where appropriate.
9. Appeal where permitted.

Moderators MUST record reasons for material decisions.

A moderator with a relevant personal or commercial conflict MUST disclose it and MUST NOT independently determine the affected case.

## 6. Moderation Actions

Depending on severity and authority, permitted actions MAY include:
- No action with recorded reasons.
- Request for clarification.
- Content correction.
- Content removal.
- Content visibility restriction.
- Warning.
- Temporary community restriction.
- Removal from a specific activity.
- Referral to the parent company's formal conduct process.

Permanent organisational membership suspension or termination MUST follow the applicable organisational governance and agreement procedures.

Community moderators MUST NOT independently terminate a Store Owner's commercial agreement unless that authority is expressly assigned.

## 7. Urgent Safety and Security Cases

Reports involving credible threats, suspected fraud, exposed personal information or security vulnerabilities MUST be escalated promptly.

The platform SHOULD support temporary protective restrictions where authorised and necessary.

Evidence MUST be preserved according to applicable data governance and retention requirements.

Security incidents MUST also follow the Platform Incident Management and Service Disruption Communication Standards.

## 8. Disputes Between Store Owners

The parent company SHOULD provide a structured route for community-related disputes.

The process SHOULD include:
- Separate statements from involved parties.
- Review of relevant evidence.
- Conflict-of-interest assessment.
- Mediation where appropriate.
- A documented outcome.
- An appeal route where applicable.

The community process MUST NOT replace legal remedies or formal contractual dispute procedures.

Commercial disagreements between independent businesses MUST NOT automatically become parent-company decisions.

## 9. Appeals

Where an appeal is permitted, the affected member MUST be able to submit reasons and supporting evidence.

An appeal SHOULD be reviewed by someone other than the original decision-maker.

The appeal outcome and reasons MUST be recorded.

## 10. Privacy and Confidentiality

Reports and moderation records MUST be restricted to authorised personnel.

The identity of a reporting member MUST NOT be disclosed unnecessarily.

The parent company MUST NOT publish moderation evidence or private disputes as community learning material without an independent lawful basis and appropriate authorisation.

## 11. Moderator Authority

Moderators MUST operate within permissions assigned through unified organisational RBAC.

The system MUST distinguish:
- Community content moderation.
- Programme administration.
- Organisational conduct enforcement.
- Security incident response.
- Formal contractual authority.

One permission MUST NOT automatically imply another.

## 12. Transparency and Consistency

The parent company MUST publish accessible community rules.

Material rule changes MUST be communicated to members.

Moderation decisions MUST apply published rules consistently.

The parent company SHOULD periodically review moderation trends to identify recurring problems and improve community safeguards.

## 13. Acceptance Criteria

The policy is satisfied when:

1. Members can report inappropriate conduct.
2. Reports have traceable review and resolution records.
3. Moderators can act only within assigned authority.
4. Conflicted reviewers are excluded from independent decisions.
5. Urgent cases have an escalation route.
6. Disputes and appeals have documented procedures.
7. Private moderation records remain protected.
8. Community moderation cannot silently override contractual governance.
9. Members can access the published community rules.
10. All moderation actions are auditable.
