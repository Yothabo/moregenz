# Store Owner Community and Entrepreneur Development Programme

## 1. Purpose

The parent company MUST operate a structured community programme that helps Store Owners develop as entrepreneurs, build professional relationships, exchange practical knowledge and learn from one another's business experiences.

The programme MUST connect Store Owners across all participating stores and locations without weakening individual store ownership, confidentiality or commercial independence.

## 2. Programme Components

The programme MUST support these components:

1. **Owner meetups:** Regular in-person or virtual gatherings.
2. **Peer networking:** Structured opportunities for owners to introduce their businesses, exchange contacts and build professional relationships.
3. **Business showcases:** Owners MAY present business achievements, challenges, experiments and lessons.
4. **Entrepreneurial challenges:** Organised activities that develop business, marketing, customer service, financial and operational skills.
5. **Competitions and recognition:** Approved competitions with published rules, judging criteria and prizes where applicable.
6. **Knowledge sharing:** A permanent digital library containing approved lessons, event notes, presentations, recordings and practical resources.
7. **Peer collaboration:** Opportunities for owners to identify partnerships, exchange services and collaborate voluntarily.

## 3. Programme Frequency

The parent company SHOULD organise:

- Monthly virtual or local networking sessions.
- Quarterly themed workshops, business showcases or competitions.
- At least one annual community gathering where operationally and financially practical.

The programme administrator MUST publish the schedule, format, location or meeting link, intended audience and registration requirements.

Events MAY be adjusted according to participation, budget and geographic distribution.

## 4. Participation and Governance

- Every active Store Owner MUST be eligible to participate.
- Participation MUST be voluntary unless a specific activity is part of an independently agreed programme.
- The parent company MUST appoint a programme administrator.
- Store Owners MAY propose topics, speakers, challenges and activities.
- The parent company MUST publish participation rules and any competition criteria before an activity begins.
- Selection for awards MUST follow documented and consistent criteria.
- Participation MUST NOT determine a Store Owner's access to unrelated platform services or commercial rights.

## 5. Knowledge Sharing and Library

The application MUST provide a Store Owner Community knowledge area.

It MUST support:
- Event records and summaries.
- Meeting notes and key lessons.
- Approved presentations and learning materials.
- Recordings or links where available and authorised.
- Business case studies and owner-submitted lessons.
- Challenge briefs, submissions and results.
- Search, categories, tags and publication dates.

Each published resource MUST record its title, content type, author or contributor, publication date, related event or topic, approval status and visibility scope.

The programme administrator MUST be able to draft, review, publish, update, archive and correct resources.

Attendance MUST NOT be required to access resources approved for general Store Owner distribution.

## 6. Event Records and Follow-up

Each event MUST have a record containing:
- Event title and purpose.
- Date, format and location or meeting link.
- Organiser and facilitators.
- Intended participants.
- Registration and attendance records where appropriate.
- Agenda and learning objectives.
- Published notes and resources.
- Follow-up actions and responsible persons.

Private discussions MUST NOT automatically become public knowledge-base content.

Contributors MUST be informed when their statements, materials or business information are intended for publication.

## 7. Privacy and Commercial Boundaries

The programme MUST protect:
- Private customer and order information.
- Store-specific financial records.
- Supplier agreements and confidential pricing.
- Business strategies identified as confidential.
- Personal contact information.

Store Owners MUST choose what business information they disclose during peer activities.

The platform MUST NOT expose one store's private dashboard, customer records, orders, inventory or financial information to another Store Owner through community features.

Published case studies MUST have contributor approval and MUST remove confidential information unless explicit permission has been granted.

## 8. Competitions and Recognition

Every competition MUST define:
- Purpose and eligible participants.
- Entry requirements and submission deadline.
- Judging criteria and scoring method.
- Judges and conflict-of-interest controls.
- Prize or recognition details, if any.
- Result publication and dispute process.

Competitions SHOULD reward measurable learning, improvement, creativity, collaboration and responsible business practices.

The programme MUST NOT reward misleading claims, manipulation of customer activity or disclosure of confidential information.

## 9. Application Integration

The application MUST provide:
- Community programme overview.
- Upcoming and past events.
- Registration and attendance status.
- Event agendas and published resources.
- Knowledge library search.
- Competition information and submissions.
- Announcements and participation updates.

Access MUST follow the unified organisational RBAC and permission evaluation specifications.

Store Owners MUST access community resources according to their active organisational membership and the resource's visibility scope.

The parent company MUST retain administrative control over programme configuration, moderation and publication.

## 10. Programme Measurement

The parent company SHOULD measure:
- Event participation and repeat attendance.
- Store Owner engagement.
- Knowledge resources published and accessed.
- Peer collaboration initiated.
- Competition participation.
- Feedback and satisfaction.
- Reported business improvements attributable to shared learning.

Metrics MUST be used to improve the programme, not to create unsupported performance rankings between stores.

## 11. Acceptance Criteria

The programme specification is satisfied when:

1. Store Owners can discover and register for eligible events.
2. Administrators can organise events and maintain event records.
3. Approved notes and resources remain accessible after events.
4. Owners who did not attend can access resources within their authorised scope.
5. Knowledge contributions have review and publication controls.
6. Competitions have documented rules and transparent judging criteria.
7. Confidential store information remains isolated.
8. Community permissions use the unified organisational RBAC system.
9. Programme activity can be reported without exposing private store data.
