## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how participating stores receive platform support, report problems, request assistance and escalate unresolved issues.

It establishes support responsibilities while preserving the distinction between platform-level services and individual store operations.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to support involving:

- Store access and accounts.
- Storefront connectivity.
- Product catalogue access.
- Inventory and stock operations.
- Orders and fulfilment.
- Customer management.
- Financial records and reconciliation.
- Integrations and notifications.
- Platform availability.
- Security and privacy incidents.
- Store onboarding and configuration.
- Training and operational guidance.

## 3. Support Principles

Support must be:

- Accessible through documented channels.
- Proportionate to issue severity.
- Assigned to an appropriate owner.
- Recorded where operationally material.
- Respectful of store autonomy.
- Limited to authorised information and actions.
- Escalated when risk or authority requires it.
- Followed through to resolution or an agreed next step.

Support must not create an informal route around security, financial or governance controls.

## 4. Support Responsibilities

### 4.1 Platform Owner

The Platform Owner is responsible for platform-level support coordination, including:

- Shared platform services.
- Store onboarding support.
- Platform administration.
- Escalation of cross-store issues.
- Platform-level provider relationships.
- Material platform incidents.
- Governance and policy interpretation.

### 4.2 Store Admin

The Store Admin is responsible for ordinary store-level support, including:

- Employee assistance.
- Store operational procedures.
- Store product and inventory questions.
- Order and fulfilment coordination.
- Customer service within the store.
- Escalation of issues requiring platform intervention.

### 4.3 Store Employees

Employees must report issues through their Store Admin or the approved support route.

They must not attempt actions outside their assigned permissions to resolve an issue.

### 4.4 Technical Support

Technical support personnel may investigate and resolve authorised technical issues.

Access to production data must be purpose-limited and follow the applicable access and audit requirements.

## 5. Support Categories

### 5.1 Access Support

Includes login problems, account status, membership, permissions and access recovery.

### 5.2 Technical Support

Includes application errors, API failures, frontend issues, connectivity and performance.

### 5.3 Operational Support

Includes product management, inventory, orders, customer workflows and platform procedures.

### 5.4 Financial Support

Includes transaction references, reconciliation questions, settlement discrepancies and financial record investigations.

Financial support does not automatically authorise financial adjustments or postings.

### 5.5 Security and Privacy Support

Includes suspected unauthorised access, data exposure, credential compromise and privacy concerns.

These matters must be escalated promptly through the Incident Response Playbook.

### 5.6 Business Support

May include operational guidance, marketing coordination, performance reviews and growth support where those services are available and agreed.

Business support must not be represented as a guaranteed financial or commercial outcome.

## 6. Support Channels

The platform must define and communicate approved support channels.

Possible channels include:

- Platform support portal.
- Dedicated support email.
- Approved business messaging channel.
- Direct escalation to the Platform Owner for urgent matters.
- Incident reporting route.

The actual channels, operating hours and response expectations remain subject to platform decisions.

Sensitive credentials and unnecessary customer information must not be submitted through ordinary support channels.

## 7. Support Request Information

A support request should include:

- Store name or identifier.
- Requester and role.
- Issue category.
- Description of the problem.
- Date and approximate time.
- Affected workflow.
- Expected behaviour.
- Observed behaviour.
- Relevant error message.
- Steps to reproduce.
- Business impact.
- Relevant reference identifiers.

Requests should avoid including passwords, access tokens, payment credentials or unnecessary personal information.

## 8. Issue Classification

Support issues should be classified according to impact and urgency.

| Priority | Description | Typical handling |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Major security, financial integrity or platform-wide service impact | Immediate escalation |
| High | Significant store disruption or critical workflow unavailable | Urgent investigation |
| Medium | Partial disruption with a workable alternative | Prioritised support |
| Low | Minor issue, general question or non-urgent request | Normal support queue |

Classification must consider actual impact rather than the requester's preferred priority.

## 9. Critical Issues

Critical issues include:

- Suspected cross-store data exposure.
- Compromised privileged accounts.
- Unauthorised financial activity.
- Widespread payment integrity problems.
- Major platform outage.
- Significant loss or corruption of business data.
- Active security exploitation.

These issues must be escalated immediately through the incident process.

Support personnel must not delay protective action while waiting for ordinary support approval where an authorised emergency response is necessary.

## 10. Support Lifecycle

A support request should follow this lifecycle:

1. Submission.
2. Acknowledgement.
3. Classification.
4. Assignment.
5. Investigation.
6. Escalation where necessary.
7. Resolution or agreed workaround.
8. Verification with the affected party.
9. Closure.
10. Follow-up where required.

Requests must not be marked resolved solely because they have been assigned or referred to another person.

## 11. Escalation Levels

### 11.1 Level 1: Store Support

The Store Admin handles ordinary store questions and employee issues.

### 11.2 Level 2: Platform Support

Issues requiring platform configuration, shared services or central technical assistance are escalated to platform support.

### 11.3 Level 3: Specialist Support

Complex technical, security, financial or provider issues are referred to an appropriate specialist.

### 11.4 Level 4: Governance or Incident Authority

Issues involving material risk, disputed authority, cross-store impact or critical incidents are escalated to the relevant platform governance or incident authority.

Escalation levels describe the support path, not a requirement to delay urgent action.

## 12. Escalation Triggers

Escalate when:

- The issue exceeds the current support person's authority.
- The issue affects multiple stores.
- A critical business workflow is unavailable.
- Security or privacy may be compromised.
- Financial records or transactions may be incorrect.
- The issue remains unresolved after reasonable investigation.
- A provider must intervene.
- A policy or governance decision is required.
- The proposed resolution could affect another store.

## 13. Store-Specific Support

Store-specific support must respect the store's operational boundaries.

A support person must not change store pricing, inventory, employee access, customer records or financial information without appropriate authority.

Where a support request requires an operational decision, the Store Admin remains responsible unless authority has been formally delegated.

## 14. Shared Platform Support

Shared platform issues must be assessed for their impact across participating stores.

The Platform Owner or authorised delegate should coordinate:

- Impact identification.
- Store communication.
- Technical investigation.
- Provider escalation.
- Workaround coordination.
- Recovery verification.

A shared issue must not be treated as isolated merely because only one store reported it.

## 15. Technical Investigation and Access

Support access to production systems must be:

- Authorised.
- Limited to the issue being investigated.
- Proportionate to the required task.
- Time-bound where practical.
- Recorded.
- Revoked when no longer needed.

Sensitive customer, employee and financial information should receive additional protection.

Support personnel must not use production data for unrelated testing or personal purposes.

## 16. Financial Support Boundaries

Financial support may investigate discrepancies and provide explanations.

The following activities require the relevant financial authority and controls:

- Posting adjustments.
- Issuing refunds.
- Changing settlement records.
- Approving repayments.
- Waiving obligations.
- Changing commission terms.
- Authorising capital or stock assistance.

Investigation and proposed correction must remain distinct from authorised financial execution.

Original transaction records must be preserved.

## 17. Security and Privacy Escalation

Suspected security or privacy incidents must be reported immediately.

The incident response process determines containment, investigation, notification and recovery.

Support personnel must:

- Preserve relevant evidence.
- Avoid unnecessary access to exposed data.
- Avoid forwarding sensitive information.
- Record relevant references and timestamps.
- Follow authorised containment instructions.

Legal and regulatory notification decisions must receive appropriate professional review.

## 18. Provider Escalation

Where an issue depends on an external provider, the responsible platform or store authority must coordinate the provider escalation.

Records should include:

- Provider.
- Relevant transaction or event reference.
- Time of occurrence.
- Provider response.
- Investigation status.
- Expected next action.

Uncertain payment or transaction outcomes must be verified before retrying an operation that could create a duplicate.

## 19. Communication and Updates

Support updates should be clear and factual.

Where appropriate, communicate:

- Current status.
- Known impact.
- Investigation progress.
- Workaround.
- Required action.
- Expected next update.
- Resolution.

Do not promise a resolution time that has not been established.

Material shared incidents must be communicated to affected stores through an approved channel.

## 20. Support Records

Support records should contain:

- Request identifier.
- Store.
- Requester.
- Category and priority.
- Assigned owner.
- Relevant timestamps.
- Investigation notes.
- Escalation history.
- Actions taken.
- Approval references.
- Resolution.
- Closure confirmation.

Records must follow applicable retention, privacy and audit requirements.

## 21. Service Expectations

Support availability and response targets must be defined separately from technical platform availability.

The platform must not imply guaranteed response or resolution times until they have been formally approved and operationally supported.

Any future service commitments should define:

- Support hours.
- Response targets.
- Escalation contacts.
- Service exclusions.
- Provider dependencies.
- Planned maintenance arrangements.

## 22. Complaints and Disputes

A store may challenge a support outcome or raise a complaint.

The complaint should be recorded and referred to an authority not directly responsible for the disputed decision where practical.

Disputes involving financial obligations, commercial terms or alleged misconduct must follow the relevant governance and agreement processes.

## 23. Support Quality Review

Support quality may be assessed using:

- Request volume.
- Issue categories.
- Response and resolution trends.
- Repeated incidents.
- Escalation frequency.
- Store feedback.
- Reopened requests.
- Outstanding actions.

Metrics must not encourage premature closure or discourage legitimate escalation.

## 24. Exceptions

Exceptions must be documented and approved by the relevant authority.

Urgent security and incident response must follow the applicable emergency procedures.

No exception may permit unauthorised access or financial activity.

## 25. Open Decisions

The following matters require further definition:

- Official support channels.
- Support operating hours.
- Response and resolution targets.
- Support staffing and ownership.
- Ticketing and record management system.
- Escalation contact structure.
- Provider support arrangements.
- Store support service commitments.
- Complaint review authority.
- Support reporting frequency.
