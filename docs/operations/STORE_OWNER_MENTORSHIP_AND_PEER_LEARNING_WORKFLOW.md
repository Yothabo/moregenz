# Store Owner Mentorship and Peer Learning Workflow

## 1. Purpose

The parent company SHOULD facilitate voluntary mentorship and peer-learning relationships between Store Owners.

The programme MUST help participants develop practical entrepreneurial skills through shared experience, guidance, accountability and knowledge exchange.

Mentorship MUST complement, not replace, formal professional, legal, financial or technical advice.

## 2. Participation

The programme MUST support two voluntary participation paths:

- **Mentor:** A Store Owner or approved external contributor willing to share relevant experience.
- **Mentee:** A Store Owner seeking guidance in a defined business area.

A participant MAY act as both mentor and mentee in different relationships.

Participation MUST NOT automatically grant organisational authority or access to another store's private records.

## 3. Mentor Profiles

Mentors SHOULD provide:
- Areas of business experience.
- Topics they can support.
- Relevant practical experience.
- Preferred mentoring format.
- Availability.
- Geographic or virtual participation preferences.
- Any relevant qualifications they choose to disclose.

The platform MUST distinguish self-declared experience from verified qualifications.

## 4. Mentee Requests

A mentorship request MUST identify:
- The business area requiring support.
- The intended development outcome.
- The preferred format.
- Expected duration.
- Relevant availability.
- Any specific matching preferences.

Requests MUST NOT require disclosure of confidential business information.

## 5. Matching and Approval

The programme administrator MAY recommend mentor matches using:
- Relevant experience.
- Development objectives.
- Availability.
- Business category.
- Location or virtual preference.
- Potential conflicts of interest.

Both participants MUST accept a proposed match before a mentorship relationship becomes active.

The system MUST record the basis and status of the match.

Neither participant MUST be forced to accept a proposed relationship.

## 6. Mentorship Lifecycle

The system MUST support:

1. Requested.
2. Matching.
3. Awaiting acceptance.
4. Active.
5. Paused.
6. Completed.
7. Cancelled.

Each relationship MUST record:
- Mentor membership.
- Mentee membership.
- Agreed objectives.
- Start date.
- Review date.
- Expected duration.
- Meeting format.
- Status.
- Completion outcome.

Either participant MAY request a pause or termination.

## 7. Mentorship Sessions

Participants SHOULD agree on a practical session plan.

Session records MAY include:
- Date and format.
- Topics discussed.
- Agreed actions.
- Progress against objectives.
- Next session date.

Private session notes MUST remain accessible only to authorised participants and administrators with a legitimate assigned purpose.

A participant MUST NOT publish another person's private session notes without permission.

## 8. Peer Learning Groups

The programme MAY establish small groups around shared business-development needs.

Examples include:
- New business owners.
- Marketing and customer acquisition.
- Financial discipline.
- Operations and fulfilment.
- Digital tools and online selling.
- Leadership and business growth.

Each group SHOULD have a defined purpose, facilitator, participation rules and review date.

Group membership MUST NOT expose private store records.

## 9. Safeguards

The programme MUST:
- Require voluntary participation.
- Prohibit harassment, coercion and exploitation.
- Provide a reporting and escalation route.
- Address conflicts of interest.
- Protect confidential information.
- Avoid presenting personal experience as guaranteed business advice.

Mentors MUST NOT use programme access to pressure mentees into purchases, investments, supplier arrangements or unrelated commercial commitments.

External professionals MUST be identified accurately and MUST operate within their actual qualifications and authorisations.

## 10. Knowledge Capture

The programme SHOULD capture general lessons that may benefit other Store Owners.

Before publication, the contributor MUST approve the proposed content and its intended visibility.

Published learning material MUST be reviewed through the community knowledge-base workflow.

Private mentorship conversations MUST NOT automatically become shared knowledge resources.

## 11. Application Requirements

The application MUST support:
- Mentor profile registration.
- Mentee requests.
- Matching recommendations.
- Mutual acceptance.
- Relationship status and objectives.
- Session scheduling records.
- Progress and completion records.
- Reporting and moderation.

The system MUST use existing organisational identities, permissions, notification services and audit records.

## 12. Measurement

The parent company SHOULD measure:
- Active mentorship relationships.
- Match acceptance rate.
- Relationship completion rate.
- Participant feedback.
- Progress against agreed objectives.
- Knowledge resources produced.

Measurements MUST assess programme usefulness without exposing private conversations or creating unsupported performance rankings.

## 13. Acceptance Criteria

The workflow is acceptable when:

1. Mentors and mentees can voluntarily register.
2. Matching considers relevant objectives and availability.
3. Both parties accept before a relationship becomes active.
4. Either party can pause or end participation.
5. Private session records remain protected.
6. Mentorship activity is auditable.
7. General lessons can be shared with contributor approval.
8. Conflicts and misconduct can be reported.
9. Mentorship does not grant access to private store data.
10. All permissions follow unified organisational RBAC.
