# Store Owner Community Event Planning and Delivery Procedure

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines how the parent company plans, approves, organises, delivers and evaluates Store Owner community events.

It applies to monthly networking sessions, quarterly workshops, business showcases, competitions and annual gatherings.

## 2. Event Planning

The programme administrator MUST maintain a forward event calendar.

Every proposed event MUST define:
- Purpose and expected outcomes.
- Event type and format.
- Intended audience.
- Proposed date and duration.
- Venue or virtual meeting arrangements.
- Facilitator and organiser.
- Capacity and registration requirements.
- Estimated costs and funding source.
- Accessibility and participation requirements.
- Learning capture arrangements.

Events MUST align with the Store Owner Community and Entrepreneur Development Programme.

## 3. Event Approval and Budget

The event organiser MUST submit the event proposal for approval before making financial commitments.

Approval MUST follow the parent company's delegated financial authority.

The proposal MUST identify:
- Venue and equipment costs.
- Travel or accommodation support, if offered.
- Catering and refreshments.
- Facilitator or speaker fees.
- Competition prizes.
- Marketing and production costs.
- Contingency allowance.

The approved budget MUST be recorded against the event.

The organiser MUST NOT exceed the approved budget without additional authorisation.

Participation fees, where applicable, MUST be disclosed before registration.

## 4. Event Formats

The programme MAY use:

- In-person meetups.
- Virtual networking sessions.
- Hybrid gatherings.
- Workshops and training sessions.
- Business showcase events.
- Peer-learning circles.
- Business competitions.
- Annual community gatherings.

The selected format MUST account for Store Owners in different locations.

Virtual participation SHOULD be provided where practical so distance does not unnecessarily exclude members.

## 5. Programme and Agenda

Every event MUST have a published agenda.

An agenda SHOULD allocate time for:
- Welcome and introductions.
- Business updates or showcases.
- Main learning topic.
- Peer discussion.
- Networking activity.
- Questions and answers.
- Agreed actions and closing.

The organiser MUST identify session facilitators and their responsibilities.

The agenda SHOULD favour practical participation over lengthy presentations.

## 6. Registration and Invitations

The application MUST display:
- Event purpose and description.
- Date, time and time zone.
- Format and venue or meeting link.
- Registration deadline.
- Capacity and eligibility.
- Participation costs, if any.
- Agenda.
- Cancellation and attendance instructions.

The system MUST confirm successful registration.

Where capacity is limited, registration MUST follow a documented allocation or waiting-list process.

Changes to event details MUST be communicated to registered participants.

## 7. Venue and Logistics

For physical events, the organiser MUST confirm:
- Venue availability.
- Capacity and seating.
- Equipment and connectivity.
- Accessibility.
- Safety and emergency arrangements.
- Catering arrangements where applicable.
- Signage and registration facilities.

For virtual events, the organiser MUST confirm:
- Meeting platform.
- Access permissions.
- Participant capacity.
- Audio and video arrangements.
- Facilitator access.
- Backup meeting arrangements.

## 8. Speakers and Facilitators

Speakers and facilitators MUST receive a clear brief covering:
- Event purpose.
- Audience.
- Session topic.
- Expected duration.
- Required materials.
- Recording arrangements.
- Confidentiality expectations.

Speaker biographies and promotional material MUST be approved before publication.

The parent company MUST disclose any material commercial relationship relevant to a speaker's presentation.

## 9. Event Delivery

The organiser MUST:
1. Confirm the venue or virtual meeting.
2. Prepare the registration and attendance process.
3. Brief facilitators.
4. Confirm materials and equipment.
5. Welcome participants.
6. Deliver the published agenda.
7. Manage time and participation.
8. Record agreed follow-up actions.
9. Close the event and communicate next steps.

Attendance MUST be recorded separately from registration.

Any attendance corrections MUST identify the person making the correction.

## 10. Photography, Recording and Consent

Participants MUST be informed before an event if photography, audio recording or video recording will occur.

Consent MUST be obtained where required.

Participants MUST have a reasonable way to communicate that they do not wish to appear in promotional material.

Recordings MUST NOT be published automatically.

The organiser MUST identify which materials may be shared with:
- Registered participants.
- All active Store Owners.
- External audiences.

External publication MUST receive separate approval.

## 11. Post-Event Activities

Within a reasonable published timeframe, the organiser MUST:
- Mark the event as completed.
- Finalise attendance records.
- Publish approved notes.
- Upload authorised learning materials.
- Publish recordings where approved.
- Record follow-up actions.
- Collect participant feedback.
- Reconcile event expenditure.

Where no recording exists, the organiser MUST still arrange a written summary for eligible events.

## 12. Cancellation and Changes

The organiser MUST record the reason for cancellation or material changes.

Registered participants MUST receive timely notifications.

Where money has been collected, refunds or alternative arrangements MUST follow the applicable commercial and payment policies.

Cancelled events MUST remain in the event history.

## 13. Event Evaluation

The organiser SHOULD evaluate:
- Registration and attendance.
- Participation and engagement.
- Achievement of learning objectives.
- Participant feedback.
- Event expenditure against budget.
- Knowledge resources produced.
- Recommended improvements.

Annual gatherings SHOULD include a review of the community programme's progress and priorities for the next year.

## 14. Application Integration

The event management workflow MUST use:
- Unified organisational membership.
- Unified organisational RBAC.
- Platform notification services.
- Community event and attendance records.
- Community knowledge-base publication workflow.
- Audit and reporting services.

The event module MUST NOT create a separate Store Owner identity system.

## 15. Acceptance Criteria

The procedure is satisfied when:

1. Events have documented proposals and approval.
2. Budgets are authorised before commitments.
3. Owners can discover and register for events.
4. Capacity and registration rules are enforced.
5. Event changes reach affected participants.
6. Attendance is recorded accurately.
7. Recording and publication respect consent.
8. Approved event knowledge is captured for non-attendees.
9. Expenditure and outcomes can be reviewed.
10. Event history remains available after completion or cancellation.
