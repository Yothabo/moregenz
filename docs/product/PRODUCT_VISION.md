## 1. Vision

Build a centrally operated retail platform that enables multiple stores to establish, operate and grow their own brands while benefiting from shared technology, infrastructure, business support and coordinated growth initiatives.

The platform must combine the capabilities of a retail commerce system with the operational support and oversight required to help participating stores succeed.

It must support independent store operations without fragmenting the underlying platform, data infrastructure or governance.

## 2. Product Purpose

The platform exists to:

- Provide a shared digital commerce infrastructure for participating stores.
- Give each store the tools to manage its products, pricing, inventory, orders and customers.
- Preserve store identity, product ownership and operational independence.
- Give the Platform Owner visibility into platform-wide activity and store performance.
- Coordinate marketing, growth initiatives and business support.
- Provide structured processes for performance reviews, audits and operational interventions.
- Support considered decisions about stock assistance and business capital.
- Maintain accountability through reliable records, permissions and financial transparency.

The platform is both a technology product and a centrally managed business operation.

## 3. Target Participants

### 3.1 Platform Owner

The Platform Owner owns and governs the central platform.

The platform must provide the tools to:

- Manage participating stores and their lifecycle.
- Administer platform-level users, access and policies.
- Monitor platform-wide activity and performance.
- Review individual store performance and operational health.
- Coordinate marketing and growth initiatives.
- Conduct audits and manage interventions.
- Review requests for stock or capital assistance.
- Monitor platform income, costs and obligations.

### 3.2 Store Admins

Store Admins are responsible for operating their assigned stores.

The platform must allow them to:

- Manage their store's identity and public-facing presence.
- Create and manage authorised product records.
- Set store-specific prices and promotions.
- Manage inventory and stock movements.
- Process and manage orders.
- Manage employees and delegated permissions.
- Review store performance and financial records.
- Request platform support and business assistance.
- Respond to platform reviews and dispute relevant decisions or records.

### 3.3 Store Employees

Store employees perform duties delegated by their Store Admin.

Their access must be controlled by assigned roles and permissions. They must not automatically receive Store Admin or platform-level authority.

### 3.4 Customers

Customers interact with individual store experiences.

The platform must provide a clear and reliable shopping experience while preserving the identity of each participating store.

## 4. Core Value Proposition

### For the Platform Owner

- Centralised oversight across participating stores.
- Consistent operational standards and governance.
- Visibility into performance, financial activity and business risks.
- A structured way to support and grow participating stores.
- Shared infrastructure without requiring each store to build its own commerce platform.

### For Store Admins

- Access to shared commerce infrastructure.
- Control over day-to-day store operations.
- Independent store branding and customer relationships.
- Tools for product, pricing, inventory and order management.
- Access to coordinated marketing and business support.
- Transparent performance and financial reporting.

### For Customers

- Distinct store identities and shopping experiences.
- Clear product information and pricing.
- Reliable ordering and order communication.
- Consistent customer service standards.

## 5. Product Principles

The platform must follow these principles:

1. **One central platform:** Participating stores operate within one centrally governed platform.
2. **Store independence:** Each store retains control over its authorised day-to-day operations.
3. **Equal treatment:** Yotaabo is a participating store and must follow the same applicable store-level rules as other stores.
4. **Clear ownership:** Product ownership, product information, store availability and inventory must be distinguishable.
5. **Controlled sharing:** Sharing a product does not transfer ownership or grant unrestricted editing rights.
6. **Financial separation:** Store financial activity and platform financial activity must be distinguishable and traceable.
7. **Accountability:** Important actions, decisions, interventions and financial changes must be recorded.
8. **Transparency:** Store Admins must be able to review relevant store records and raise documented disputes.
9. **Human oversight:** Material decisions about funding, suspension and other significant interventions require authorised human review.
10. **Proportionate governance:** Platform oversight must protect the wider business without unnecessarily controlling ordinary store operations.
11. **Scalability:** The platform must support additional stores without requiring a separate platform for each one.
12. **Maintainability:** The system should favour clear, modular architecture and avoid unnecessary complexity.

## 6. Core Product Capabilities

The product is expected to provide the following capability areas:

### 6.1 Store Management

- Store onboarding and approval.
- Store configuration and branding.
- Store lifecycle management.
- Store-level administration.
- Store-specific operational settings.

### 6.2 Identity and Access Management

- Platform-level administration.
- Store-level administration.
- Employee accounts and delegated roles.
- Invitations and access lifecycle management.
- Permission enforcement and security controls.

### 6.3 Product Catalogue

- Product creation and management.
- Product ownership and origin tracking.
- Controlled product sharing.
- Store-specific product availability.
- Store-specific commercial information.

### 6.4 Commerce Operations

- Pricing and promotions.
- Inventory management.
- Stock movements and adjustments.
- Shopping and checkout.
- Order processing and fulfilment.
- Returns, cancellations and refunds.

### 6.5 Financial Management

- Store revenue and expenses.
- Platform commissions and expenses.
- Commission calculations and settlements.
- Stock supplied and capital advanced.
- Repayments and outstanding obligations.
- Financial reporting and documented disputes.

### 6.6 Performance and Business Support

- Store performance monitoring.
- Operational and financial reporting.
- Performance reviews and action plans.
- Marketing and growth support.
- Support requests.
- Stock and capital assistance reviews.
- Audits and interventions.

### 6.7 Governance and Accountability

- Platform policies.
- Approval workflows.
- Audit records.
- Decision and amendment history.
- Store responses and disputes.
- Suspension and closure procedures.

## 7. Long-Term Direction

The platform should evolve into a scalable environment where additional stores can join and operate without compromising the independence of existing stores.

Future development may include:

- More advanced performance analysis.
- Coordinated campaigns across participating stores.
- Improved customer retention capabilities.
- More detailed inventory and demand analysis.
- Structured stock and capital assistance programmes.
- Expanded operational reporting.
- Additional integrations that support commerce and business operations.

These are strategic directions, not commitments that every capability must be delivered in the initial release.

## 8. Success Measures

The platform's success should be evaluated using measurable outcomes, including:

### Platform Growth

- Number of approved and active stores.
- Store onboarding and activation rates.
- Store retention and closure rates.
- Platform-wide sales and order activity.

### Store Performance

- Store revenue and gross margin.
- Order volume and fulfilment performance.
- Inventory accuracy and stock turnover.
- Returns, cancellations and customer retention.
- Progress against agreed performance targets.

Store performance must be assessed in the context of each store's circumstances, operating history, product category and agreed targets. Comparisons between stores should account for relevant differences and must not replace individual performance reviews.

### Operational Effectiveness

- Time required to onboard a store.
- Time required to process and fulfil orders.
- Accuracy of operational and financial records.
- Resolution time for support requests and disputes.
- Frequency and impact of operational issues.

### Platform Sustainability

- Platform income and operating costs.
- Commission collection and settlement accuracy.
- Cost of supporting participating stores.
- Outstanding financial obligations.
- Sustainability of any stock or capital assistance programme.

Success measures must be defined consistently and must not be treated as automatic approval or suspension rules.

## 9. Product Boundaries

The platform is not intended to:

- Give the Platform Owner unrestricted control over every ordinary store action.
- Make all stores use the same public-facing brand.
- Treat shared product availability as a transfer of product ownership.
- Merge all store inventory into one undifferentiated stock balance.
- Conceal platform charges or financial calculations from affected Store Admins.
- Automatically approve funding or impose material interventions based only on performance scores.
- Assume that unresolved commercial, legal or accounting arrangements have already been decided.

## 10. Strategic Constraints

Product development must respect the following constraints:

- Existing storefront and administrative functionality must be protected during migration and expansion.
- New capabilities must preserve existing data integrity.
- Store separation must be enforced through application permissions and data access controls.
- Platform-wide reporting must not expose information to unauthorised store users.
- Financial records must support traceability and reconciliation.
- Security, privacy and applicable South African legal and accounting requirements must be considered.
- Architecture must support future growth without introducing unnecessary infrastructure complexity.
