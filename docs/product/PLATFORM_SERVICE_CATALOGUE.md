## 1. Purpose

This document defines the services the central platform makes available to participating stores.

It establishes service categories, ownership boundaries, responsibilities and the distinction between standard platform capabilities and additional support arrangements.

The catalogue describes the intended service model. It does not imply that every service is already implemented or available.

## 2. Service Principles

Platform services must:

- Support participating stores without removing their operational autonomy.
- Apply consistent platform-wide standards.
- Respect store ownership and data boundaries.
- Have identifiable responsibilities and escalation paths.
- Be measurable where practical.
- Distinguish standard services from optional assistance.
- Avoid promising capabilities that have not been implemented or agreed.
- Maintain clear financial attribution where services incur costs.

## 3. Service Categories

The platform services are grouped into the following categories:

1. Core platform services.
2. Store onboarding services.
3. Product and catalogue services.
4. Technical and integration services.
5. Operational support services.
6. Business development and marketing support.
7. Performance and review services.
8. Financial and stock assistance.
9. Security and continuity services.

## 4. Core Platform Services

The platform provides shared software capabilities required to operate participating stores.

These include:

- Store administration capabilities.
- Identity and access management.
- Shared product catalogue infrastructure.
- Store-specific product listings.
- Inventory management capabilities.
- Order and commerce processing.
- Customer management capabilities.
- Financial recordkeeping capabilities.
- Notifications and supported integrations.
- Reporting and performance information.
- Audit and operational records.

Each store accesses these capabilities within its authorised scope.

Shared infrastructure does not mean that store records or commercial operations are shared indiscriminately.

## 5. Store Onboarding Services

The platform coordinates the onboarding of approved stores.

Onboarding services may include:

- Store creation.
- Store identity and configuration setup.
- Store Admin account provisioning.
- Initial permission assignment.
- Store-specific integration package generation.
- Frontend developer handover.
- Product catalogue setup.
- Inventory preparation.
- Integration configuration.
- Operational readiness checks.
- Store activation coordination.

Store creation must automatically initiate the required store-specific integration package provisioning process.

The package must provide the public configuration and developer information needed to connect an independently developed frontend to the shared backend and catalogue.

Private credentials remain protected within authorised server-side systems.

## 6. Product and Catalogue Services

The platform provides shared catalogue infrastructure and manages product ownership and availability boundaries.

Services may include:

- Product source record management.
- Product ownership tracking.
- Store product listing management.
- Product availability controls.
- Product classification and organisation.
- Product information validation.
- Catalogue search and retrieval.
- Product media management.
- Catalogue integration support.

A store remains responsible for its own prices, commercial terms and inventory.

Permission to sell a product does not automatically grant permission to modify its source information.

## 7. Technical and Integration Services

The platform provides technical services required for participating stores to connect to shared infrastructure.

These may include:

- API access.
- Store-scoped integration configuration.
- API documentation and developer guidance.
- Supported SDKs or starter tooling.
- Integration health monitoring.
- Approved webhook processing.
- Domain and CORS configuration support.
- Integration troubleshooting.
- Credential rotation and revocation procedures.

The platform controls backend security and store-context enforcement.

Frontend developers must not receive privileged database or backend credentials for public storefront use.

## 8. Operational Support Services

The platform may coordinate operational support for participating stores.

Support may include:

- Platform usage guidance.
- Store administration assistance.
- Product and catalogue support.
- Inventory process guidance.
- Order workflow support.
- Integration troubleshooting.
- Incident coordination.
- Operational documentation.
- Escalation of issues requiring platform authority.

Support does not automatically grant platform personnel unrestricted access to store data.

Any exceptional support access must be authorised, purpose-limited, recorded and revoked when no longer required.

## 9. Business Development and Marketing Support

The platform may provide business development and marketing support to participating stores.

Potential services include:

- Marketing planning.
- Campaign coordination.
- Promotional strategy.
- Brand and storefront guidance.
- Product presentation advice.
- Customer acquisition initiatives.
- Growth planning.
- Marketing performance reviews.
- Shared promotional opportunities.

Campaign participation remains subject to store agreement and applicable campaign conditions.

Platform campaign approval does not automatically enrol a store or authorise communication to its customers.

## 10. Performance and Business Reviews

The platform may provide structured performance reviews to help stores understand their operations and identify improvement opportunities.

Reviews may consider:

- Sales and revenue trends.
- Product performance.
- Inventory availability.
- Order fulfilment.
- Customer activity.
- Marketing exposure.
- Operational efficiency.
- Agreed business targets.
- Store operating history.

Comparisons must account for relevant differences between stores.

Performance information must be explainable and must not independently trigger financial assistance, penalties or other material interventions.

## 11. Financial and Stock Assistance

The platform may consider additional assistance for participating stores.

Potential assistance may include:

- Stock supplied by the platform.
- Capital advances.
- Agreed financial support.
- Shared promotional funding.
- Other approved business assistance.

Such assistance is not guaranteed or automatically included in standard platform services.

Every arrangement must have an authorised decision, documented terms, clear ownership and financial treatment.

Terms may include:

- Amount or stock quantity.
- Purpose.
- Ownership.
- Repayment or settlement conditions.
- Applicable obligations.
- Supporting evidence.
- Approval authority.
- Review arrangements.

No default commission rate, capital advance rate or repayment schedule is established by this document.

## 12. Security and Continuity Services

The platform is responsible for coordinating shared security and continuity capabilities.

These may include:

- Identity and access controls.
- Platform monitoring.
- Security incident coordination.
- Backup and recovery arrangements.
- Integration credential management.
- Audit records.
- Service availability monitoring.
- Disaster recovery coordination.

Stores remain responsible for following security procedures and protecting their own authorised accounts and devices.

## 13. Service Ownership

Each service must have an identifiable operational owner.

Platform-owned services are managed centrally.

Store-owned activities remain the responsibility of the relevant Store Admin and authorised employees.

Where a service involves both parties, responsibilities must be documented so that neither side assumes the other is accountable.

## 14. Service Availability and Support Expectations

Service availability, response targets and support hours must be defined through approved operational policies or agreements.

This document does not establish guaranteed uptime, response times or resolution deadlines.

Support priority should consider:

- Security impact.
- Number of affected stores.
- Customer impact.
- Financial exposure.
- Business interruption.
- Availability of workarounds.
- Criticality of the affected service.

## 15. Service Costs and Commercial Treatment

The financial treatment of platform services must be transparent.

Where costs are incurred, records should distinguish:

- Platform operating costs.
- Store-specific service costs.
- Shared service costs.
- Optional support costs.
- Third-party provider charges.
- Agreed financial assistance.
- Recoverable expenses.

Any charges to participating stores must be supported by an approved commercial arrangement.

This catalogue does not create a fee obligation.

## 16. Service Changes and Retirement

The platform may introduce, modify, suspend or retire services through an authorised change process.

Material changes must consider:

- Store impact.
- Existing agreements.
- Data ownership.
- Operational dependencies.
- Integration compatibility.
- Security implications.
- Migration requirements.
- Communication and notice requirements.

Services must not be withdrawn in a way that silently destroys store records or prevents access to historical obligations.

## 17. Service Feedback and Improvement

Participating stores should have a defined way to:

- Report service problems.
- Request improvements.
- Suggest new capabilities.
- Challenge service assessments.
- Provide feedback on support quality.

Requests must be assessed against platform priorities, security, cost, operational capacity and benefits to participating stores.

A request does not automatically create an implementation commitment.

## 18. Service Limitations

The platform is not automatically responsible for every aspect of a participating store's business.

Unless separately agreed, platform services do not include:

- Guaranteed sales or profitability.
- Guaranteed customer acquisition.
- Unrestricted business financing.
- Guaranteed stock supply.
- Legal, tax or accounting representation.
- Independent management of store employees.
- Ownership of store-specific commercial decisions.
- Custom frontend development for every store.
- Unrestricted access to external provider accounts.

Any additional responsibility must be explicitly agreed and documented.

## 19. Open Decisions

The following matters require further definition:

- Which services are included in the standard participation arrangement.
- Whether optional services will carry separate charges.
- Service availability targets.
- Support hours and escalation targets.
- Service performance indicators.
- Marketing support eligibility.
- Financial and stock assistance eligibility.
- Cost allocation for shared services.
- Service review frequency.
- Formal service agreements and service-level commitments.
