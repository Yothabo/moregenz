## 1. Purpose

This document defines store personnel roles responsible for daily sales operations, order fulfilment, inventory, customer service and supplier coordination.

These roles operate within an assigned store scope. They do not grant authority over the parent company or other stores.

## 2. Sales and order operations

### 2.1 Sales Representative

**Purpose:** Handle authorised sales enquiries and sales activities.

**Responsibilities:**
- Assist customers with product enquiries.
- Record authorised sales interactions.
- Provide accurate product and pricing information.
- Escalate complaints and unusual requests.

**Authority:**
- Access assigned sales tools and permitted product information.
- Create sales records where authorised.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT change protected prices or promotions.
- MUST NOT access unrelated customer or order records.
- MUST NOT promise unapproved discounts or commercial terms.

### 2.2 Order Processing Officer

**Purpose:** Coordinate order processing after submission.

**Responsibilities:**
- Review assigned orders.
- Verify required order information.
- Coordinate payment and fulfilment status.
- Record order exceptions and customer communication.

**Authority:**
- Store-scoped order processing permissions.
- Access customer information required for assigned orders.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT alter payment verification outcomes without the required authority.
- MUST NOT delete or conceal order history.
- MUST NOT access orders belonging to another store.

### 2.3 Fulfilment Officer

**Purpose:** Prepare and dispatch authorised customer orders.

**Responsibilities:**
- Pick and pack assigned orders.
- Record fulfilment progress.
- Maintain dispatch evidence.
- Coordinate authorised delivery handovers.

**Authority:**
- Access fulfilment details for assigned orders.
- Update permitted fulfilment statuses.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT change order totals or payment records.
- MUST NOT mark an order dispatched without the required evidence.
- MUST NOT disclose customer delivery information outside fulfilment duties.

## 3. Inventory operations

### 3.1 Inventory Controller

**Purpose:** Maintain accurate store inventory records.

**Responsibilities:**
- Monitor stock levels.
- Record authorised stock movements.
- Investigate stock discrepancies.
- Maintain inventory adjustment evidence.

**Authority:**
- Store-scoped inventory permissions.
- Create and update permitted stock records.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT change another store's stock.
- MUST NOT modify financial or order records through inventory permissions.
- MUST NOT conceal unexplained stock adjustments.

### 3.2 Stocktake Officer

**Purpose:** Conduct physical stock counts and submit reconciliation results.

**Responsibilities:**
- Perform scheduled and approved stock counts.
- Record counted quantities.
- Document variances and supporting evidence.
- Submit results for review.

**Authority:**
- Read access to assigned inventory records.
- Create stocktake records and variance submissions.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT approve their own restricted stock adjustments.
- MUST NOT rewrite original count evidence after submission.
- MUST NOT change inventory outside the approved reconciliation process.

## 4. Customer support

### 4.1 Customer Support Agent

**Purpose:** Resolve customer enquiries and service requests.

**Responsibilities:**
- Respond to assigned customer enquiries.
- Assist with order-status questions.
- Record complaints and support cases.
- Escalate refunds, disputes and sensitive matters.

**Authority:**
- Access to customer and order information required for assigned cases.
- Create and update support records.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT browse customer records without a valid service purpose.
- MUST NOT disclose another customer's information.
- MUST NOT approve refunds or financial adjustments unless separately authorised.

### 4.2 Customer Support Supervisor

**Purpose:** Supervise store support operations and escalations.

**Responsibilities:**
- Allocate support cases.
- Review service quality.
- Handle escalated complaints.
- Monitor response and resolution performance.

**Authority:**
- Store-scoped support supervision permissions.
- Access to cases within the supervised scope.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT access another store's support records by default.
- MUST NOT override restricted financial or legal decisions.

## 5. Supplier and procurement operations

### 5.1 Procurement Officer

**Purpose:** Coordinate authorised purchasing activities.

**Responsibilities:**
- Prepare purchase requests.
- Obtain supplier quotations.
- Track approved purchase orders.
- Maintain procurement evidence.

**Authority:**
- Access to assigned supplier and procurement records.
- Create purchase requests within delegated limits.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT commit company or store funds without approval.
- MUST NOT approve their own restricted purchase requests.
- MUST NOT alter supplier banking details without the required verification.

### 5.2 Supplier Coordinator

**Purpose:** Maintain day-to-day supplier communication and records.

**Responsibilities:**
- Maintain supplier contact and product information.
- Coordinate delivery schedules.
- Record supplier issues.
- Escalate quality and fulfilment problems.

**Authority:**
- Access to assigned supplier records and communications.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT approve supplier onboarding or financial terms unless separately authorised.
- MUST NOT expose confidential supplier information to unauthorised stores.

## 6. Temporary and external operational access

1. Contractors, temporary workers and external service providers MUST receive individual, scoped assignments.
2. Access MUST be limited to the assigned store, task and period.
3. External access MUST have an expiry date.
4. Access to customer, payment and supplier information MUST be restricted to the minimum required.
5. Expired assignments MUST stop authorising access.
6. External personnel MUST NOT receive Store Owner or parent-company authority by implication.

## 7. Separation of duties

1. Order processing, payment verification, refund approval and financial release MUST remain distinguishable permissions.
2. Stock counting and restricted stock adjustment approval MUST be separable.
3. Purchase preparation and purchase approval MUST be separable where required by financial controls.
4. Customer support access MUST NOT imply authority to change financial outcomes.
5. Supervisory status MUST NOT automatically grant unrestricted access to all store data.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

This specification is satisfied only when:

- Operational roles are separately assignable.
- Each assignment is restricted to its store and permitted functions.
- Customer, order, inventory and supplier permissions are distinct.
- Financial and stock-control separation of duties is enforced.
- Temporary and contractor access expires as configured.
- Operational actions and sensitive record access are auditable.
