## 1. Purpose

This specification defines how the platform measures the participation, quality and business-development impact of the Store Owner Community programme.

Measurement MUST help the parent company improve the programme and allocate resources responsibly.

It MUST NOT become an unsupported ranking system or a mechanism for controlling independent Store Owners.

## 2. Measurement Domains

The system MUST distinguish these measurement domains:

1. Community participation.
2. Networking and peer relationships.
3. Event and workshop effectiveness.
4. Knowledge sharing and learning.
5. Mentorship and peer development.
6. Challenges and competitions.
7. Collaboration outcomes.
8. Programme financial performance.
9. Store Owner feedback and satisfaction.

Each domain MUST have defined metrics, data sources and reporting ownership.

## 3. Participation Metrics

The system SHOULD measure:
- Active community profiles.
- Event registrations.
- Actual event attendance.
- Repeat participation.
- Virtual and physical participation.
- Discussion participation.
- Knowledge-resource contributions.
- Challenge participation.
- Mentorship participation.

Registration MUST NOT be counted as attendance.

A profile MUST NOT be counted as an active participant merely because it exists.

## 4. Networking Metrics

The system SHOULD measure:
- Connection requests.
- Accepted connections.
- Active peer relationships.
- Collaboration opportunities published.
- Expressions of interest.
- Completed collaborations.
- Participation in structured networking activities.

The system MUST distinguish a connection from a completed collaboration.

Connection counts MUST NOT be treated as proof of business value.

## 5. Learning and Knowledge Metrics

The system SHOULD measure:
- Resources submitted.
- Resources approved and published.
- Resource review turnaround.
- Resource views and downloads.
- Search activity.
- Searches with no useful results.
- Event summaries published.
- Contributor participation.
- Member-reported usefulness.

Views and downloads MUST NOT automatically be interpreted as knowledge gained.

## 6. Mentorship Metrics

The system SHOULD measure:
- Mentorship requests.
- Accepted matches.
- Active relationships.
- Completed relationships.
- Agreed objectives.
- Participant feedback.
- Self-reported progress.

Private mentorship conversations MUST NOT be included in general management reporting.

## 7. Challenge and Competition Metrics

The system SHOULD measure:
- Challenges published.
- Eligible participants.
- Entries received.
- Completion rates.
- Judging turnaround.
- Appeals and procedural disputes.
- Lessons captured.
- Participant feedback.

Competition winners MUST NOT automatically be treated as the highest-performing businesses overall.

## 8. Business Development Impact

Where participants voluntarily provide information, the programme MAY assess:
- Improvements in business processes.
- New marketing methods adopted.
- Customer-service improvements.
- New supplier or business relationships.
- New products or services.
- Improvements in operational efficiency.
- Self-reported business growth.

The system MUST distinguish:
- Directly recorded programme activity.
- Participant-reported outcomes.
- Independently verified outcomes.

The parent company MUST NOT claim that the programme caused business growth solely because growth occurred after participation.

## 9. Financial Performance

The programme SHOULD report:
- Approved budget.
- Actual expenditure.
- Sponsorship income.
- Event costs.
- Cost per event.
- Participation costs where relevant.
- Budget variances.

Financial reports MUST follow the parent company's financial authority and confidentiality rules.

## 10. Feedback

The platform SHOULD collect feedback after:
- Events.
- Workshops.
- Competitions.
- Mentorship relationships.
- Knowledge-resource use.
- Major community activities.

Feedback forms SHOULD capture usefulness, relevance, quality and suggested improvements.

Sensitive feedback MUST be handled through restricted access.

The system SHOULD support anonymous feedback where appropriate and technically feasible.

## 11. Reporting Architecture

The platform MUST provide authorised management reports for the community programme.

Reports SHOULD support:
- Monthly participation summaries.
- Quarterly programme reviews.
- Annual impact assessments.
- Event-level reports.
- Knowledge-base reports.
- Mentorship and challenge reports.
- Programme budget reports.

Reports MUST use authoritative source records rather than manually maintained duplicate statistics.

## 12. Privacy and Data Boundaries

Community analytics MUST follow the Platform Data Governance and Information Lifecycle Policy.

Reports MUST:
- Apply role-based access.
- Aggregate information where individual identity is unnecessary.
- Exclude private messages and confidential mentorship notes.
- Avoid exposing another store's private commercial information.
- Record access to restricted reports.

Store Owners MUST only see individual-level information they are authorised to access.

## 13. Review and Improvement

The programme administrator MUST review performance periodically.

The review MUST identify:
- Activities producing useful participation.
- Activities with low engagement.
- Knowledge gaps.
- Unmet learning needs.
- Geographic participation barriers.
- Financial sustainability.
- Recommended programme changes.

Material programme changes MUST follow the applicable product requirements, approval and communication standards.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

The measurement system is acceptable when:

1. Each programme domain has defined metrics.
2. Registration and attendance are measured separately.
3. Connections are distinguished from actual collaborations.
4. Knowledge access is not misrepresented as learning outcomes.
5. Business outcomes distinguish reported from verified evidence.
6. Programme finances are traceable.
7. Feedback can be collected and analysed.
8. Management reports use authoritative source records.
9. Private store and mentorship information remains protected.
10. Reports support measurable programme improvement.
