## 1. Purpose

The knowledge base MUST preserve practical knowledge generated through Store Owner meetups, workshops, discussions, competitions and business experiences.

It MUST make approved learning materials available to authorised Store Owners, including those who did not attend the original activity.

The library MUST function as a searchable, maintained learning resource, not merely an archive of uploaded documents.

## 2. Content Categories

The library MUST support these categories:

- Business planning and entrepreneurship.
- Marketing, branding and customer acquisition.
- Sales and customer relationships.
- Financial management and cash flow.
- Operations and fulfilment.
- Product sourcing and supplier relationships.
- Digital tools and technology.
- Leadership and personal development.
- Business case studies and lessons learned.
- Event notes, presentations and recordings.
- Competitions, challenges and practical exercises.

Administrators MAY add categories without changing the underlying content model.

Resources MAY belong to multiple categories.

## 3. Knowledge Resource Types

The system MUST support:

- Written articles and guides.
- Meeting notes and summaries.
- Presentations and downloadable documents.
- Video and audio recordings.
- External learning links.
- Business case studies.
- Templates and practical worksheets.
- Question-and-answer resources.
- Challenge results and lessons.

Each resource MUST have a defined content type and storage method.

## 4. Resource Metadata

Every resource MUST maintain:

- Unique resource ID.
- Title.
- Summary.
- Main content or file reference.
- Content type.
- Category and tags.
- Author or contributor membership.
- Related event, challenge or discussion where applicable.
- Visibility scope.
- Publication status.
- Created and updated timestamps.
- Reviewer and approval timestamp where applicable.
- Version number.

Resources MUST retain their original attribution when edited by an administrator.

## 5. Submission and Publication Workflow

The workflow MUST support:

1. Draft creation.
2. Submission for review.
3. Review for relevance, accuracy, privacy and consent.
4. Approval or rejection.
5. Publication.
6. Revision and version history.
7. Archival or withdrawal.

Contributors MUST be able to see the status of their submissions.

Only authorised reviewers MAY publish resources.

A rejected submission MUST retain its review outcome and reason.

## 6. Event Notes and Learning Capture

Every eligible event SHOULD produce a documented learning record.

The event organiser MUST assign responsibility for capturing notes.

Event notes SHOULD include:
- Event purpose and agenda.
- Main discussion themes.
- Practical lessons.
- Business examples shared with permission.
- Questions and answers.
- Agreed follow-up actions.
- Recommended resources.

Notes MUST distinguish confirmed information from personal opinions and individual experiences.

Recordings MUST NOT replace written summaries entirely.

Where recordings are unavailable or consent is not granted, written notes MAY still be published.

## 7. Search and Discovery

The library MUST support searching by:
- Title and summary.
- Full-text content where technically supported.
- Category.
- Tags.
- Contributor.
- Event or challenge.
- Publication date.
- Content type.

Users MUST be able to filter results by category and content type.

Search results MUST only include resources the requesting user is authorised to access.

The system SHOULD recommend related resources using relevant categories, tags and topics.

Recommendations MUST respect the same access restrictions as direct searches.

## 8. Knowledge Quality and Maintenance

Published resources MUST have an identifiable owner responsible for accuracy and review.

The system SHOULD support:
- Review dates.
- Outdated-content warnings.
- Correction requests.
- Related-resource links.
- Duplicate-content identification.
- Version history.
- Superseded-resource references.

Material containing outdated operational, legal, financial or technical guidance MUST be reviewed before being presented as current guidance.

Archiving a resource MUST NOT silently erase its historical record.

## 9. Contribution and Recognition

Store Owners MAY contribute voluntarily.

The platform SHOULD display contributor attribution where permission has been granted.

Contributors MAY request correction or withdrawal of their contributions.

The parent company MUST define how contributor recognition works before launching awards or contribution-based incentives.

Contribution activity MUST NOT automatically grant additional organisational authority or access permissions.

## 10. Copyright, Consent and Confidentiality

Contributors MUST have the necessary rights or permissions to submit material.

The platform MUST record relevant permissions for:
- Written contributions.
- Presentations.
- Photographs.
- Audio and video recordings.
- Business case studies.
- Third-party materials.

The parent company MUST NOT assume ownership of a Store Owner's business brand, original work or commercial methods merely because they are shared in the community.

Resources MUST exclude confidential customer, supplier, employee and financial information unless a lawful and explicitly authorised purpose permits its inclusion.

## 11. Application Experience

The knowledge base MUST be accessible from the Store Owner Community area.

The interface MUST provide:
- Featured learning resources.
- Recently published resources.
- Category navigation.
- Search and filtering.
- Resource detail pages.
- Download or playback controls where authorised.
- Contributor attribution.
- Related resources.
- Submission and correction workflows for authorised contributors.

The experience SHOULD be usable on mobile devices.

## 12. Reporting

Administrators SHOULD be able to report:
- Resources submitted and published.
- Review turnaround time.
- Resource views and downloads.
- Search terms with no useful results.
- Most accessed categories.
- Outdated resources awaiting review.
- Contributor participation.

Analytics MUST NOT expose one Store Owner's private activity to another Store Owner.

## 13. Acceptance Criteria

The knowledge base is acceptable when:

1. Resources can be submitted, reviewed, published and withdrawn.
2. Every published resource has required metadata and attribution.
3. Event notes remain accessible after the event.
4. Non-attendees can access resources within their authorised scope.
5. Search and recommendations enforce visibility restrictions.
6. Resource changes preserve version and audit history.
7. Copyright, consent and confidentiality are addressed before publication.
8. Administrators can identify outdated resources.
9. Contributors can request corrections or withdrawal.
10. The library works within the existing organisational permission system.
