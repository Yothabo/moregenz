## 1. Purpose

This document defines roles responsible for central marketing, brand strategy, creative production, content, advertising and business growth.

The central team provides shared services to the parent company and participating stores. It MUST preserve each store's identity, commercial interests and approval rights.

## 2. Marketing leadership

### 2.1 Marketing Head

**Purpose:** Lead marketing strategy and agency operations.

**Responsibilities:**
- Define central marketing strategy.
- Coordinate marketing resources and campaigns.
- Manage marketing performance and budgets within delegated authority.
- Supervise marketing personnel.
- Coordinate store marketing requests.

**Authority:**
- Marketing department permissions.
- Campaign approval authority explicitly delegated to the role.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT approve the use of a Store Owner's brand or campaign where Store Owner approval is required.
- MUST NOT commit advertising expenditure beyond delegated limits.
- MUST NOT change store positioning or identity without authorised approval.

### 2.2 Marketing Strategist

**Purpose:** Develop marketing plans for the parent company and assigned stores.

**Responsibilities:**
- Research audiences and markets.
- Prepare campaign strategies and channel plans.
- Define measurable campaign objectives.
- Recommend growth opportunities.

**Authority:**
- Access to assigned marketing briefs and approved performance data.
- Create and update strategy documents.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT launch campaigns without required approvals.
- MUST NOT represent recommendations as approved Store Owner decisions.

## 3. Campaign and advertising roles

### 3.1 Campaign Manager

**Purpose:** Coordinate approved campaigns from planning through completion.

**Responsibilities:**
- Create campaign records and schedules.
- Coordinate creative, advertising and publishing tasks.
- Track approvals, delivery and results.
- Report campaign performance.

**Authority:**
- Manage campaigns within assigned store or company scope.
- Submit campaign materials for approval.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT bypass campaign approval workflows.
- MUST NOT publish unapproved store-specific content.

### 3.2 Digital Advertising Specialist

**Purpose:** Plan and operate authorised paid advertising.

**Responsibilities:**
- Configure approved advertising campaigns.
- Manage targeting, budgets and optimisation.
- Monitor advertising performance.
- Maintain expenditure and campaign evidence.

**Authority:**
- Access to assigned advertising accounts and campaign budgets.
- Execute approved advertising changes within delegated limits.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT exceed approved budgets.
- MUST NOT use a Store Owner's advertising account without permission.
- MUST NOT change campaign objectives or material claims without required approval.

### 3.3 Social Media and Community Manager

**Purpose:** Manage authorised social publishing and audience engagement.

**Responsibilities:**
- Prepare and schedule approved content.
- Manage assigned social channels.
- Respond to authorised enquiries.
- Escalate complaints and sensitive matters.

**Authority:**
- Publishing and moderation permissions for assigned channels.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT publish on behalf of a store without the required approval.
- MUST NOT claim ownership of a store's social accounts or audience.
- MUST NOT disclose private customer or business information.

## 4. Creative production roles

### 4.1 Creative Director

**Purpose:** Lead creative quality and visual direction.

**Responsibilities:**
- Define creative production standards.
- Review creative concepts and outputs.
- Coordinate designers, photographers and editors.
- Ensure work respects approved brand direction.

**Authority:**
- Creative department permissions.
- Internal creative review authority.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT override a Store Owner's approved brand direction.
- MUST NOT approve their own work where independent approval is required.

### 4.2 Brand Designer

**Purpose:** Create and maintain approved visual brand assets.

**Responsibilities:**
- Produce logos, layouts and brand materials.
- Apply approved brand guidelines.
- Maintain source files and asset versions.
- Prepare store-specific creative deliverables.

**Authority:**
- Access to assigned brand assets and creative workspaces.
- Create and revise draft assets.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT alter official store identity without approval.
- MUST NOT reuse one store's confidential or proprietary assets for another store without permission.

### 4.3 Product Photographer / Videographer

**Purpose:** Produce product and promotional photography or video.

**Responsibilities:**
- Capture products and approved campaign subjects.
- Follow technical media specifications.
- Deliver correctly formatted production assets.
- Maintain production and revision records.

**Authority:**
- Access to assigned production briefs and supplied products.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT publish captured material without approval.
- MUST NOT claim ownership of products or underlying store brand rights.
- MUST follow agreed licensing, usage and retention terms.

### 4.4 Content Creator / Copywriter

**Purpose:** Produce written and multimedia content.

**Responsibilities:**
- Prepare product descriptions and campaign copy.
- Develop scripts, captions and editorial content.
- Maintain accuracy and brand consistency.
- Submit work for review.

**Authority:**
- Create and revise content within assigned scope.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT publish unapproved claims, prices or promotions.
- MUST NOT invent product characteristics, certifications or customer endorsements.
- MUST respect store-specific voice and approved messaging.

### 4.5 Creative Editor

**Purpose:** Edit and prepare approved media for publication.

**Responsibilities:**
- Edit images and video.
- Produce platform-ready exports.
- Maintain quality and technical specifications.
- Record final asset versions.

**Authority:**
- Edit assigned source material and create deliverables.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT materially misrepresent products.
- MUST NOT remove required disclosures or alter approved claims.
- MUST NOT publish final assets without the required approval.

## 5. Growth and analytics roles

### 5.1 Growth Manager

**Purpose:** Coordinate measurable customer acquisition and business growth initiatives.

**Responsibilities:**
- Identify growth opportunities.
- Coordinate approved experiments.
- Monitor acquisition and conversion performance.
- Recommend improvements to store growth plans.

**Authority:**
- Access to assigned growth reports and approved campaign data.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT change store pricing, product availability or commercial policy through marketing permissions.
- MUST NOT run experiments that violate approved brand or customer-data rules.

### 5.2 Marketing Analyst

**Purpose:** Analyse marketing and campaign performance.

**Responsibilities:**
- Prepare campaign and channel reports.
- Analyse permitted audience and conversion data.
- Maintain reporting definitions.
- Identify performance trends and anomalies.

**Authority:**
- Read access to approved analytics within assigned scope.
- Create reports and analysis records.

**Restrictions:**
- MUST NOT access identifiable customer records unless separately authorised.
- MUST NOT export or combine store data beyond approved reporting scope.

## 6. Store approval and service boundaries

1. Every store-specific campaign MUST identify the target store and responsible Store Owner.
2. Store Owners retain approval rights defined by the campaign and creative workflows.
3. Central marketing personnel may prepare recommendations and draft materials without receiving authority to publish them.
4. Store-specific brand assets MUST remain attributed to the relevant store.
5. Shared marketing access MUST be scoped to assigned stores and services.
6. Central staff MUST NOT access a store's private customer, order or financial records merely because they manage its marketing.
7. Advertising expenditure MUST have an identified payer, approved budget and recorded authorisation.
8. Marketing service delivery and optional paid creative services MUST be distinguishable in the platform.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

This specification is satisfied only when:

- Marketing, advertising, creative and analytics roles are separately assignable.
- Every store-specific assignment has an explicit store scope.
- Drafting, review, approval and publishing are distinguishable actions.
- Store Owner approval requirements are enforced by workflow.
- Advertising budgets and expenditure are traceable.
- Creative assets retain ownership and licensing records.
- Marketing analytics access respects customer and store-data boundaries.
