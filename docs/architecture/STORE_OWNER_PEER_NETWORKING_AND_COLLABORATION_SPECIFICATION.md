## 1. Purpose

The platform MUST provide structured opportunities for Store Owners to connect, learn about one another's businesses and establish voluntary professional relationships.

The networking system MUST support meaningful connections beyond event attendance.

It MUST preserve each store's independence, commercial confidentiality and ownership boundaries.

## 2. Networking Profiles

The application MUST provide an optional community profile for each participating Store Owner.

The profile MUST be linked to the person's canonical organisational membership.

It SHOULD include:
- Display name.
- Profile image.
- Business or store name.
- Business description.
- Business category.
- Operating location or service area.
- Products or services offered.
- Business website or approved public links.
- Areas of experience.
- Topics the owner can help others with.
- Topics the owner wants to learn about.
- Preferred collaboration interests.

Store Owners MUST control which optional profile fields are visible to other community members.

Private account details, authentication information and internal organisational records MUST NOT appear on community profiles.

## 3. Business Discovery

The community MUST allow authorised members to discover participating businesses.

Discovery SHOULD support filters for:
- Business category.
- Location or service area.
- Products and services.
- Skills and expertise.
- Collaboration interests.
- Business topics.

Search results MUST respect profile visibility settings.

The platform MUST NOT expose private store analytics, sales figures, customer records, supplier contracts or internal performance assessments.

## 4. Connection Requests

Store Owners MAY send connection requests to other Store Owners.

The system MUST support:
- Sending a request.
- Accepting a request.
- Declining a request.
- Cancelling a pending request.
- Removing an existing connection.
- Blocking or reporting inappropriate behaviour.

A connection MUST NOT automatically grant access to another store's private information.

Connection status MUST be explicit and auditable.

The platform MUST prevent duplicate active requests between the same members.

## 5. Peer Communication

Connected members MAY communicate through approved platform channels.

Where direct messaging is provided, it MUST:
- Identify the participating members.
- Enforce sender and recipient permissions.
- Support blocking and reporting.
- Protect private conversations from unrelated Store Owners.
- Follow the platform's data retention and privacy policies.

The system MUST NOT assume that connecting through the community authorises marketing messages to customers or access to another owner's customer base.

## 6. Expertise and Peer Learning

Store Owners SHOULD be able to identify members who:
- Have experience in a particular business area.
- Have completed a relevant challenge.
- Have published an approved case study.
- Have offered to mentor or assist other owners.
- Are seeking advice or collaboration.

Expertise labels MUST represent self-declared or appropriately verified information.

The platform MUST distinguish experience claims from formal professional qualifications.

## 7. Collaboration Opportunities

The system SHOULD support voluntary collaboration listings.

A listing MUST contain:
- Title and description.
- Participating business or member.
- Collaboration type.
- Required skills or resources.
- Location or service area where relevant.
- Visibility scope.
- Contact or expression-of-interest method.
- Status and expiry date.

Supported collaboration types MAY include:
- Joint promotions.
- Shared learning activities.
- Supplier referrals.
- Business partnerships.
- Cross-promotion.
- Event participation.
- Skills exchange.
- Product or service collaboration.

The parent company MUST NOT represent a proposed collaboration as an approved partnership unless it has separately authorised that arrangement.

## 8. Introductions and Networking Activities

Community administrators SHOULD be able to organise:
- Structured introductions during events.
- Business spotlight sessions.
- Peer discussion groups.
- Topic-based networking circles.
- Voluntary mentoring introductions.
- Location-based networking sessions.

Participants MUST be informed about the purpose and format of each activity.

Introductions MUST respect participant consent and profile visibility.

## 9. Collaboration Lifecycle

The system SHOULD support these states:

1. Draft.
2. Published.
3. Open for interest.
4. Discussion in progress.
5. Active.
6. Completed.
7. Cancelled.
8. Expired.

A Store Owner MUST be able to withdraw their participation in a voluntary collaboration.

The platform MUST retain appropriate historical records without presenting an inactive collaboration as current.

## 10. Moderation and Conduct

Community administrators MUST be able to review reported profiles, connection requests, messages and collaboration listings within their authorised scope.

The system MUST support:
- Reporting inappropriate conduct.
- Reviewing reports.
- Recording moderation decisions.
- Restricting abusive accounts where authorised.
- Appealing moderation decisions.
- Preserving relevant audit evidence.

Moderation MUST follow the Store Owner Conduct, Compliance and Enforcement Policy.

## 11. Permissions and Data Boundaries

The networking system MUST use unified organisational RBAC.

Store Owners MUST only manage their own community profiles and voluntary listings, subject to approved permissions.

Community administrators MAY moderate content within their assigned authority.

No community connection or collaboration MUST grant access to another store's:
- Customer records.
- Orders.
- Payments.
- Inventory.
- Financial reports.
- Private supplier records.
- Administrative settings.

## 12. Reporting

The parent company SHOULD measure:
- Active community profiles.
- Connection requests and accepted connections.
- Participation in networking activities.
- Active collaboration listings.
- Completed collaborations.
- Member-reported usefulness.

Reports MUST focus on community development and MUST NOT be used to create unsupported commercial rankings.

## 13. Acceptance Criteria

The networking system is acceptable when:

1. Store Owners can create and manage optional community profiles.
2. Members can discover businesses using authorised profile information.
3. Connection requests have controlled lifecycle states.
4. Connections do not grant private store-data access.
5. Collaboration opportunities can be published and managed.
6. Introductions and peer activities respect consent.
7. Reporting and moderation workflows are available.
8. Networking permissions use unified organisational RBAC.
9. Historical collaboration records remain distinguishable from active opportunities.
10. All protected actions are enforced by the backend.
